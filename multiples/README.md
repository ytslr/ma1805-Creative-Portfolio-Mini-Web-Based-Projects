You need to use google authenticator to log on to moodle and campus connect every time after it automatically signs out after some time. It might feel annoying sometimes since you have to do that really often, and sometimes you just do no have your phone with you.

I remember clicking the wrong number for so many times over and over by mistake to the point the method got temporarily unavailable. I had to check something on moodle and I was frustrated because of all the extra steps. 

The user can choose from the three numbers randomly generated. The text reads "Tap the number shown in your other device" but there is no other device, nor a right answer. The website will randomly generate more numbers after every choice, and an attempt counter is displayed below.
